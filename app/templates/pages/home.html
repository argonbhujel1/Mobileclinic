Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a repair with pickup?

Go to Book a Repair, describe the problem, set your pickup address on the map. Our team (admin) picks up, repairs, and can deliver back. Track status anytime.

Do you serve Urlabari?

Yes — Mobile Clinic is focused on Urlabari, Morang and nearby areas for phones, exchange and repair coordination.

How does phone exchange work?

Open Exchange listings, pick a phone, submit your device details and photos. The seller and admin are notified. You can also list your phone for exchange from the seller dashboard.

COD and QR payment?

Many listings support COD and QR. QR may require a minimum amount; the seller/admin confirms the order after verifying payment.

How do I track my order or repair?

Use Track in the menu with your name, mobile and email — no order number required. Repair tickets also have a tracking link from email.

Can I sell my used phone?

Register, become a seller, upload your payment QR, then list the phone with photos. Platform commission applies on successful sales as per terms.

Is there a warranty on second-hand phones?

Warranty depends on each listing (seller or shop warranty). Always read the product description before buying.

How do I contact you on Facebook or Instagram?

Use the Facebook / Instagram icons in the footer (set by admin) for direct message, or the green WhatsApp button on the right.
